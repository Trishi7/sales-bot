# Wording for the sheet — AI news (8 Oct 2026)

For Vaishnavi. Plain words, ready to paste. Nothing here changes when the news is posted; only how it looks and what
Saley does when somebody asks for it.

## Bot Rules tab — Rule 1 (AI news), column "What the Bot Shares / Checks"

> Today's AI news worth reading — the industry, and our own PoCs. One post every weekday at 2 PM with up to 5 stories
> since the last post (Monday's covers the weekend). First the top 2 stories about people on Outreach PoCs or companies
> on Master Pipeline / Outreach PoCs, each marked with its sheet row, e.g. "(Synthflow AI — on Master Pipeline)"; then
> the best of everything else. Every news message looks the same: a bold heading with the day, then at most 5 stories,
> each a bold headline with a link to the outlet. Major stories that didn't fit follow straight away in one "More AI
> News" message (major stories only, at most 5), which doesn't count toward the 5 posts a day. Asked for the news in the
> channel, Saley gives the 5 best stories it hasn't sent before, newest first, and repeats a story only when nothing new
> is left. Never held back, never rolled to the next day. Checked again every hour, weekends included, and posted
> straight away only when something is major. No 2 PM post on Saturday or Sunday. Nobody on the departures list is
> looked up.

## Global Rules tab — row "AI news"

## What a news message looks like now

```
**AI News, Thu 8 Oct**

- **State AGs launch investigations into OpenAI AI safety** ([Reuters](link))

- **TM Forum and Accenture launch AI trust framework for telecoms** ([techcrunch.com](link))
```

- The same shape for the 2 PM post, "More AI News", "Breaking AI News" and an answer to "any AI news?".
- A story about one of our PoCs keeps its fuller line, as before: the headline, what happened, and whose it is —
  "(Synthflow AI — on Master Pipeline)".
- Never more than 5 stories in one message.

## What changed from the old wording

- "More AI news today" is now "More AI News, <day>", and carries only major stories (it used to carry anything worth
  posting, up to 8).
- An answer in the channel no longer says when the news is posted or what was "already posted", and does not repeat
  what it has already given.

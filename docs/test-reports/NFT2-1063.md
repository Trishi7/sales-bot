## 9. Findings

### 9.1 `wording.declined(why, [], what)` (closed: the built line is pinned)
Plan section 10 said `Leaving {what} as it is — {why}. ...`; that sentence fails `wording.register_problems` (R-a, "it is"), so
the planner and builder ruled for `{Label} is off — {why}. Nothing has changed in the sheet.` and the lead said to pin it.
`tests/test_replies.py` pins the built line; the two-argument output is unchanged word for word. It is on the human's
sign-off list (Q8) and is one line in `wording.py`.

### 9.2 The on-demand text of an unsent post and the post itself (resolved)
First build: the intro line (R3's three `EVENT_OPENERS`, R4's "N open:" variants, R13's) was a random draw at send time
and a seeded one on demand, so they could differ; I compared everything but the intro. Plan section 20 (B1) then made the
sender seed its wording by (day, slot) too. The tests now compare the WHOLE block, intro included (`o_objectives...`,
`e3`, `e3b`, replay step 4) and `b1` shows two sends of one (day, slot) under different random states read the same.

### 9.2b Known limit (builder's)
An already-sent post's stored text can include send-time research (for example a research note line), which the on-demand
answer would never add for an unsent post. So the 13:58 and 14:05 objectives answers can differ for such a post; the tests
use posts with no research item (past days, R3/R13).

### 9.3 Questions that survive into the answer
The objectives answer is the posts, so it carries their own lines: R4's "Anything here already done? Say which and
I'll take it off." and R10's "Fill them in and I'll pick it up next Monday." (a forward-looking schedule phrase, though
none of the plan's forbidden words). No proposal stands behind either. A "sure" to the answer is acknowledged with a
reaction, a "yes" goes on to the normal path with the answer as context; nothing is written either way. For the human to
decide whether those two sentences belong in an answer that "never mentions when" anything happens.

### 9.4 Things the plan or the old scripts got wrong, and the fixes
- Plan 13.2 says the replay's step 1 gains "NOTHING_TODAY since the queue is empty". True only if the sheet is stubbed;
  without it the new tool reads the real sheet and the offline guard (correctly) ends the run with exit 3. The step-1
  world now installs an empty offline sheet (`replies_world.Sheet`). Same for `verify_profile_lookup.py` T4.
- Pretending a day AFTER the real date makes R3's research note "(14 Oct hasn't happened yet, so there is nothing to
  research)" appear in a sent post but not in the template, so my objectives worlds use PAST days (Wed 30 Sep, Mon 28 Sep).
- Three settings leaked from the laptop's `.env` into the sweep: `SALES_ASK_CHANNEL_ID`, `SALES_DIGEST_CHANNEL_ID`,
  `WEEKLY_DIGEST_CHANNEL_ID` pick the channel `_maybe_send_drip` posts to; now pinned in `replies_world.PINS`.
- The replay's line `SKIP rule 13 replies proper ... owned by NFT2-1063` is mislabelled: plan 5.6 builds the hook as a
  stub and gives the replies to the Rule 13 owner (RULE13.md section 14). The stub exists, returns False and does
  nothing (case `h`). I left tester-r13's line as it is.
- Windows: `python -m replies` died printing the 👍 on a cp1252 console on the first run; fixed by the builder.
- The one "planner made no R3 post" failure I saw (twice, in my own table-generator, never in a test run) is explained: the
  generator was started from a different working directory, where the relative `bot_rules.yaml` path and `.env` do not
  resolve, so no rules load. Run from the repo root (as every verify script does) it never recurs. Not a flake in the product.
- **Date-dependent replay (found 8 Oct, fixed).** `7 Oct: the answer came from todays_news` started failing when the real clock
  rolled to 8 Oct. Cause: my harness, not the product. `World.real_clock()` called `clock.forget()`, which only drops the cache;
  the pretend day (Wed 7 Oct, set while sending R3's post) is stored in the world's DB and came straight back, so `todays_news`

  the fix `oct7()` gave `answer_has_stories False` on 8 Oct; after, True. The other cases pin their day (Wed 7 Oct, Wed 30 Sep,
  Mon 28 Sep, Sat 3 Oct, or the patched real clock in `n1`) or run on the real day with stories seeded relative to now, so none
  depends on the wall date. Re-run on 8 Oct: `verify_replay_oct6.py` ALL PASSED, `verify_replies.py` ALL PASSED, whole pytest
  1 failed (known-red) / 874 passed / 25 skipped.

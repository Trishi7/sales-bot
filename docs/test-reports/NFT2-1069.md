## 8. Known limits, and questions for the human

Each is built the way the prompt said, or the way the code already behaved. None blocks the release; each is a
decision a person may want to make differently.

1. **What does not fit by 20:00 waits for its rule's next day, not for tomorrow.** The prompt said it "rolls to the
   next weekday as it does today". Today nothing is stored: the next day's queue is worked out afresh from the
   rules that run that day, so Monday's closure support (R10), if it did not go, comes back the next Monday. On a
   normal day this never happens (the longest order is four posts and there are four slots). It happens after an
   outage or a late start: with the first post at 17:20 only one more goes that day. *Question: should a post that
   missed its day be sent first thing on the next weekday? That needs a small stored record and a rule for where
   it sits in the next day's order.*
2. **A stretch of news can fall between two AI news posts.** "Since the previous post" is R1's slot in the previous
   day's order (as specified). On a day a rule ahead of R1 had nothing to say, R1 really posts earlier than that
   slot: a Monday with no deliverables posts the news at 16:00, and Tuesday's post covers from Monday 18:00. The
   stories collected between 16:00 and 18:00 on Monday are in neither post. A major one still reaches the channel
   through the hourly check, and all of them are given to anybody who asks for the news. *Question: accept this,
   or measure from 14:00 (the earliest R1 can ever post)? That loses nothing, at the cost of yesterday afternoon's
   unposted stories being considered again the next day.*
3. **Posts leave on a 15-minute check, so "14:00" is 14:00 to 14:15.** That is how every post has always gone out
   (Rule 13's 15:00 too). With ticks landing on the quarter hours, as in the test, the times are exact.
   *Question: is up to 15 minutes late acceptable, or should the check interval be lowered (say 5)?*
4. **14:00 is no longer anybody's news time, and it is still not an hourly-check slot.** `NEWS_CHECK_TIMES` skips
   14:00 because the main post used to go then. Nothing is missed (the 15:00 check looks back to 13:00), but a major
   story that breaks at 13:10 is posted at 15:00, not 14:00. *Question: add 14:00 to `NEWS_CHECK_TIMES`? It is a
   one-line `.env` change and I left the default alone.*
5. **R11's limit went from 3 to 10 companies a post.** With a weekly post, a fourth company left out would be past
   the 7-day window by the next Wednesday and never asked about. An eleventh in one week still is. *Say if 10 is
   wrong.*
6. **A Wednesday the bot is down loses that week's new companies** (they are older than the window by the next).
   Unchanged in kind: a missed day already lost a day-old company; it is a week's worth now.
7. **R7 lists a DM'd person whose row says Connected but has no LI Connected Date.** Rule 13 skips such a row (and
   reports it as a gap), so it is not "covered" and R7 picks it up. That follows the prompt's definition
   ("Connected with an LI Connected Date"). On 7 Oct every DM'd row was Connected with a date, so on that sheet R7
   would have listed nobody.
8. **`MEETING_DAYOF_TIME` now times every meeting post**, not only the day-of touch. The name is kept (no new
   variable); the comments say what it does.
9. **A weekly rule's post reads as a "second ask" every other week.** The re-ask clock sees "asked 7 days ago,
   nothing came back". This was already so for the Monday and Thursday rules; R7 and R11 now join them. Not changed.

lines (Mon: R4, R7, R1, R10 · Tue: R5, R6, R2, R1 · Wed: R11, R1, R3 · Thu: R5, R1, R12 · Fri: R2, R6, R1), the line
`Drip: up to 5 message(s) per weekday ..., first at 14:00 IST, then every 120 min (plus up to 0), nothing after
20:00`, and no `MESSAGE_JITTER_MINUTES` warning. If instead it says `[rules] ... could not be loaded`, nothing will
post until the rules file is fixed; the line names the rule and both lists.

"At 14:00" below means the first check at or after 14:00: up to 15 minutes later.

**The first Monday**

| Time | Expect |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | A meeting-prep post for every meeting on Outreach PoCs that is today, in 3 days or in 5 days, and a follow-up for any completed meeting with a blank Notes/Remarks that is due. All together. **Nothing at all if there is no such meeting.** No other post before 14:00 |
| 14:00 | "This week's deliverables": the open P1 items due this week or overdue. With it, once, the pending-approvals list if any approval is waiting. **If no P1 is open there is no deliverables post, and everything below moves up one slot** (R7 at 14:00, AI news at 16:00, closure support at 18:00, nothing at 20:00) |
| 15:00 | "Next steps": up to 5 connected contacts, one line each. Whatever went at 14:00, and whatever goes at 16:00, is not moved by it |
| 16:00 | "DM sent, no meeting yet": people DM'd more than 7 days ago with no meeting, at most 5. **Check every name against the sheet: none of them may have "Sid - LI Addition" = Connected together with an LI Connected Date.** If every DM'd person is Connected with a date (as on 7 Oct) there is no such post, and AI news is at 16:00 and closure support at 18:00 |
| 18:00 | "AI News, Mon <date>": at most 5 stories, the top PoC stories first, covering the weekend (from Friday 18:00). A "More AI News" message may follow at once, major stories only |
| 20:00 | "Closure support", or the line saying Prospect Status and Closure Prob% are empty. Nothing after it |
| Any time | A reminder somebody set, at its minute. A "Breaking AI News" post only if something major happened |

Then check: between any two of the 14:00 / 16:00 / 18:00 / 20:00 posts there are at least 120 minutes; no post from
that list arrived after about 20:15; "what are today's objectives?" lists the same posts.

| 14:00 | "New in the pipeline": every company that appeared on Master Pipeline since the previous Wednesday's post, whatever day it appeared (up to 10), asking whether to look for PoCs. Nothing is searched until an approver says yes. **Not on the first Wednesday after a fresh database** (the first run only records the tab). **If no company is new, AI news is at 14:00 and events at 16:00** |
| 15:00 | "Next steps", as every weekday |
| 16:00 | "AI News, Wed <date>", covering from Tuesday 20:00 |
| 18:00 | "AI events and summits": what falls in the next 14 days, ending with the offer to remind again. Silent in a week with no event close enough to mention and nothing new found |
| 20:00 | Nothing. Wednesday has three posts in its order |

Also on any day that week: a company added to Master Pipeline on Thursday must NOT be mentioned on Friday, Monday
or Tuesday, and must be in the next Wednesday's 14:00 post. On Tuesday the AI news is the last post, at 20:00. On
Sunday: one deliverables post at 14:00 only if a P1 is due Monday; otherwise nothing. Saturday: nothing.

**In a test day or a simulation** (`make it Monday`, `simulate week`): the same posts in the same order; each carries
the `[TEST...]` tag and nothing else differs.
